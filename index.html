Who runs it

A three-person board composed of members with a database research background.

Hannes Mühleisen Chair|Co-creator of DuckDB

Co-created DuckDB at CWI Amsterdam in 2018. Former professor of data systems at Radboud University.

Mark Raasveldt Board|Co-creator of DuckDB

Co-created DuckDB at CWI Amsterdam in 2018. Obtained his PhD in database systems at CWI’s Database Architectures group.

Peter Boncz Board|Database systems researcher

CWI researcher and professor at VU Amsterdam, architect of influential analytical database management systems.
